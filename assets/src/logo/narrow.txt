# narrow: the condensed alphabet for the CORRUPT logo, drawn by hand, one
# character a pixel of the 480x270 frame (█ ink, · ground).
#
# Tall terminal letters, cap height 40: stems 6 wide, bars 5 rows (top
# 0-4, middle 17-21, bottom 35-39), square inside, the outermost corners
# bevelled by one pixel. S and G carry short spurs at their terminals.
# The A's top is cut at 45 degrees and the R's bowl steps in over its leg,
# so the two never meet. The script tears these letters apart; drawn
# plain and square, they survive it. Only the letters of SIGNAL OVERDRIVE
# are drawn.
